# Breaking version upgrades

Guidelines for upgrading across breaking releases, one page per major line. Each entry says what
changed, who is affected and what to do about it.

## [2.0.0](?path=/docs/migration-faq-breaking-version-upgrades-2-0-0--docs)

2.0.0 renders the redesigned Ring design system by default, draws its icons with lucide, removes `ChipsInput` and re-namespaces the `data-testid` attributes.

`ChipsInput` and the icons have their own pages: [ChipsInput Migration](?path=/docs/migration-faq-breaking-version-upgrades-2-0-0-chipsinput-migration--docs) and [Icon Map](?path=/docs/migration-faq-breaking-version-upgrades-2-0-0-icon-map--docs).
